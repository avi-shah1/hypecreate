## 2. How it works (3 steps, use this wording)
1. **Save:** securely connect your bank accounts (via Plaid) to track your savings.
2. **Play:** pick your lucky numbers. The more you save, the more tickets you earn for the weekly draw.
3. **Win:** match the most winning numbers to win cash.

## 5. Trust & security (key objection handlers)
- **Read-only access:** Lodavo sees balances to award tickets and **cannot move, hold or withdraw money**.
- Bank connection via **Plaid** (thousands of US banks); bank logins never pass through Lodavo.
- Bank-grade encryption.
- **Provably fair draws:** winning numbers can't be changed, and anyone can verify a draw.
- Disconnect anytime. **Does not sell user data.**

## 6. Business model
- 100% free, no entry fees.
- Revenue comes from **referral fees** when users choose financial products Lodavo suggests. This funds the prize pool at no cost to the user. Users who decline keep earning tickets.
- Prize funding is also described as coming from partnerships, sponsors and promotional campaigns.

## 7. Audience
- Younger everyday savers: students, Gen Z and young adults building first savings habits (press: "young Canadians", "the next generation").
- Founder framing: an alternative to "brain rot" scrolling and to gambling-style apps where young people lose money. Lodavo motivates better saving habits and includes daily savings tips.
- Research note: demand for prize-linked savings is highest among people with low savings, which matches the "I'm broke / paycheck to paycheck" audience in our Oriane trend data.

## 8. Brand voice
Upbeat, playful, rewarding, gamified, but always paired with **safety + transparency**. Casual, Gen-Z friendly, never preachy. Excitement (winning) + reassurance (your money never leaves your bank).
