# Lodavo: rules for any copy the tool writes

This file is the brand input to the script and caption prompts. Another brand would be another file in `brand/`.

## What it is

A Canadian prize-linked savings app, not a bank. Users connect their own chequing or savings account. Their savings stay in their own account. Saving earns free tickets for a weekly cash draw.

## Facts that must be right

- Every $25 you have saved earns a free ticket, every week. $1,000 saved is 40 tickets every week without doing anything.
- Choose 7 numbers (0 to 99) by Monday 8 PM ET. One winning number is revealed every night at 8 PM ET, Monday through Sunday.
- Most matches wins the $100 weekly prize. 7 of 7 wins the $10,000 jackpot. So at least $100 is won every week.
- Open to legal residents of Canada at the age of majority.
- Bonus tickets exist: daily spin, streaks, referrals, partner rewards.

## How it talks

Like a friend. Short sentences, lots of "you". Sell the fun: free tickets for saving, a chance to win cash every week, a number revealed every night. Mention how the bank connection works at most once in a video, never in a hook or a call to action.

The setup step is "connect your chequing or savings account" or, shorter, "connect your bank account".

## Never say

- "bank" or "savings account" to describe Lodavo itself: say "savings app"
- "safe", "risk-free", "insured", "guaranteed". "You can't lose your money" is fine and literally true for Lodavo, which holds no money and charges nothing: use it against gambling or lotteries, and "your savings stay in your own bank account" elsewhere.
- "18+": say "age of majority"
- "gambling", "betting", "wager", and "lottery" unless comparing against one
- "win prizes" without "can win" or "chance to win"
- "pick" numbers: say "choose"
- "entries": say "tickets"
- "&": write "and"
- moving money "into Lodavo": people save in their own account
- "Lodavo sees, watches, knows or tracks your savings": say "you can track your savings"
- "compounds", "returns", "interest", "at risk": Lodavo pays no interest
- "a real shot" or "a free shot": say "a chance"
- "members": say "users"
- em dashes

## People

- Real winners and reviews only. Written names look like "Kyle C. · QC": first name, last initial, two-letter province, no city.
- An AI-generated person never plays a user or a winner.

## Legal line

If a video mentions prizes, winning or the jackpot, this line shows for its last 3 seconds, word for word:

- EN: `No purchase necessary. STQ required. Rules & odds: lodavo.com/rules`
- FR: `Aucun achat requis. QH requise. Règles & chances : lodavo.com/regles`

## Language

Each video is English or French, never both. Quebec French, "tu" for casual content. Non-breaking spaces in figures and before colons (`2 000 $`).
